## What `0x5b9f90` is

`0x5b9f90..0x5c56b0` (46,881 B, end-exclusive `0x5c56b1`) is the debug-menu page builder that
runs inside the Init `0x531d60` post chain. Measured from the pinned ELF
(`local/re/eboot.elf`, sha256 `d0723afb…5861d3`) by `tools/lift/slice_5b9f90.py`:

- 7,645 objdump-aligned instructions (the earlier handover quoted 7,685; that count included the
  eight bytes past the body end),
- exactly one `ret` (`0x5c56aa`) and exactly one backward branch
  (`0x5c1e5b: jne 0x5c1d50`, 33 iterations),
- 1,136 direct calls to **25 distinct targets** and 53 indirect calls,
- 491 calls into the constructors already modelled by `fmenu_ctors_slot306`
  (`0x26c10` group ×17, `0x26f90` command ×40, `0x26db0` named ×41, `0x27120` float-from-var ×53,
  `0x26ea0` toggle ×8, `0x26b60` separator ×7, `0x27050` float ×4) plus 305 `0xf590` releases,
  170 `0x272d0` destroys and 94 `0x20d60` registrations.

It is a straight-line page tree: each cluster writes two or three `SharedStr` views into stack
slots and calls one constructor. That is why it decomposes cleanly; the four rare callees and the
one loop nest are the only irregular boundaries.

## Executed this turn

`native/core/init_5b9f90_slices.{hpp,cpp}` executes in body order:

- **S0** — canary store, group `"Shadow"` under `"CP10"` through `0x26c10`, `0x268b0` root
  registration, two `0xf590` view releases;
- **S1/S2** — the two page builders `0x2170` ("Shadow Misc") and `0x5cf140` as **named host
  contracts**; their bodies are not lifted and their slices are not counted as executed;
- **S3.1/S3.2** — the two boss pages: group + 16 command items each (`0x26f90`, target tags
  `0x2fed5b8` / `0x2fed6f8`, commands `"0".."15"`, flags `0x30`), registered in the same order and
  released per cluster.

Totals: **3 groups, 32 command items, 35 registrations, 70 releases, 2 contracts, canary stored,
3 executed slices = 5,412 of 46,881 B (11.5 %)**. Every item tuple is byte-pinned: 32 call sites
resolve to `0x26f90`, and each cluster is checked for its caption/command literal, the target-tag
`lea` and the `0x30` flags store.

The 34 items live in host-owned storage (`Init5b9f90Hooks::item_storage`, 40 slots) because the
original builds them in its own 0x27a0 stack frame; the first fixture run proved the point by
walking a dangling root list after the storage was a local.
