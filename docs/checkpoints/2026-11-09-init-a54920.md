- **Inference:** none of this advances a runtime gate. `0xa55050` is a contract, not a lifted body,
  and no boot, audio, save or Metal validation was performed. G1 remains **PARTIAL**.
- **Inference:** the body’s `snprintf` sizes (`0x11/0x12/0x10` and `0xc/0xd/0x15`) are the size
  arguments, not format lengths; the fixture pins the resulting truncation.
